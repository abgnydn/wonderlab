To swap it out, drop your own kid-safe instrumental in this folder
(any name → just rename to loop.mp3 OR change MUSIC_FILE_URL in
src/audio.js). Suggested CC0 / CC-BY sources:

  • Pixabay Music    — https://pixabay.com/music/   (CC0, no attribution)
  • Free Music Archive — https://freemusicarchive.org/genre/Kid-Friendly
  • Kevin MacLeod     — https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/
  • Audionautix       — https://audionautix.com/    (CC-BY 4.0)
  • Chosic            — https://www.chosic.com/free-music/all/

If you swap to something not under CC0, please update this file
and the README so the attribution stays correct.
